A *ductus* is the order and the direction of the strokes that make a letter. The app stores that, and not an outline: each glyph is a list of ordered strokes, each stroke a path of cubic Béziers with a direction. Latin print comes first; kana, hanzi, cursive and calligraphy use the same model. Designed in full, not yet built.
