Not another macro tracker. Alpha-gal syndrome is an allergy to a sugar every non-primate mammal carries, so the risk is rarely the word "beef" on the front of the package; it is gelatin, tallow and magnesium stearate in the ingredient text. The center of the service is a lexicon over that text, and the nutrient numbers follow behind it. A pilot, developed further before it goes public.
