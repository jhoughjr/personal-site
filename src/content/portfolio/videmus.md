---
title: videmus
description: An iOS and macOS app that measures how far you sit from the screen, draws at a true angular size at that distance, and simulates vision conditions through the camera.
tags: ["Swift", "iOS", "macOS", "vision"]
---

The front camera finds the distance from your eye to the screen, and the app draws at a known physical size, so a letter subtends a true angle. The screen becomes a chart that stays honest when you lean in or move back. Latin *videmus*, "we see".
